Major Projects

Logo and poster

Byaheng Lasalyano

Visual artist, LSU University Student Government and TINGOG Campus Press, 2026

Byaheng Lasalyano is a documentary-based initiative that tells the stories of Lasallian students who kept studying despite hardship. It was a collaboration between the LSU University Student Government and the TINGOG Campus Press. The tagline reads “Sugilanon sa pangandoy ug paglahutay.”

I was one of the visual artists and created the official logo and poster, working from the palette and art direction set by the Visuals Committee head. I was offered that head role but declined it, since my academic load left no room to manage a team. It was the right call. It let me put my full effort into the visuals, and it showed me that backing a good lead is a real way to contribute.

Illustration series

Bahandi Volume II: Icarian Odyssey

Illustrator, LSU TINGOG Campus Press, [year]

Bahandi is the Press’s literary folio. This volume follows the story of Icarus in four chapters: the rise, the peak, the fall, and the abyss. I made five illustrations that cover all four.

To keep all five pieces consistent, I followed the project's art direction. It also pushed me to draw with thin or no outlines, which was new to me. It was hard at first, but I use that approach in my recent work now. It showed me I can adapt to a set style and still grow, even on a major project with strict standards.

Film poster

Halwa

This is the very first film project regional competition I have joined as their poster artist and supporting member of the production. The film was nominated 3rd Place for Best Film during the Stakeholders Forum and Pelikulagaryo 2026 Regional Awarding on September 24, 2026 at The Atrium, Limketkai Mall,Cagayan de Oro.

This project challenges my way conceptualization skills where I had that goal that I'll be able to present a concept that reflects the story of and cinematography mood of the film in a way it doesn't spoil potential viewers for this film.
