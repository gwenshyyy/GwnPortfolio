ABOUT ME

Hi! I’m Gwen D. Aninao, an illustrator, and designer currently serving as an Illustrator and Cartoonist at the LSU TINGOG Campus Press. I am seeking an On-the-Job Training (OJT) opportunity where I can bring my artistic vision and design skills to a creative team.

My background in the campus press has taught me how to take complex concepts and turn them into impactful, easy-to-understand visuals under tight deadlines. While drawing is my foundation, I look at illustration from a strategic designer's perspective. I focus on how composition, color theory, and intentional storytelling can directly elevate and amplify a brand's core message.

Beyond core illustration and graphic design, I love exploring how visuals move and function across different mediums. I am actively expanding my skill set in UI/UX Design, Animation, and Brand Identity to ensure my artwork seamlessly integrates into modern digital experiences.
