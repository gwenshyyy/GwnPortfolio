ABOUT ME

Hi! I’m Gwen D. Aninao, an IT student currently serving as an Illustrator, Cartoonist, and core team member as the Associate Editor for Operations at the LSU TINGOG Campus Press.

My journey in campus journalism started in elementary school, teaching me how to translate complex concepts into clear, impactful visuals under tight deadlines. While I started as a traditional artist, joining the publication and studying IT pushed me into digital art and layout design.

Working with tools like Figma and Canva, I create digital illustrations, UI/UX designs, and brand concepts. Driven by leadership and continuous learning, I constantly adapt my art style—not only to meet publication demands, but also whenever a new trend sparks my curiosity—and I look forward to expanding my toolkit with software like Adobe.
